Options Scanner directional setups → ranked contracts + A+ grader

quant·python·garch·monte-carlo·ml
500 tickers A+ grader 0DTE + swing
// why ranked #2

The clearest statement of the methodology: "Most option scanners are noisy because they evaluate every contract on every ticker, then try to sort the firehose. This one inverts the problem." A Saty-style technical setup must fire first (Signal). Surviving tickers get GARCH-conditioned Monte Carlo P(profit), EV, and composite scoring (Rank). Now extended: an A+ Confluence Scorer re-grades survivors across 5 weighted categories (Technical, Vol/VIX, Catalyst, Macro/Breadth, Liquidity) with position-sizing discipline. Also includes intraday mean-reversion, 0DTE entry/exit gates, market regime, sector rotation, and a trade-scoring model.

Earnings Reaction Model post-earnings stock move vs. implied move

// why ranked #9

Cross-ticker ML model predicting post-earnings stock reaction — gap magnitude, direction, and post-announcement drift — benchmarked against the live implied move. Per-ticker history (~15–80 quarters) is too small for ML, so ~500 S&P names are pooled into ~20–40K events: a data-density decision that is itself the Signal-stage design. Three gradient-boosted model heads (quantile regression for magnitude, classifier for direction, regression for drift) are the Rank. An honesty gate forces the model to report "no exploitable edge found" if it cannot beat the naïve baseline out-of-sample.

DIS Bioacoustics disentangled SSL for marine novelty detection

research·ssl·resnet·contrastive·ml
225K annotations FAU DIS vision + audio
// why ranked #11

Research question: does a self-supervised encoder trained to separate what made a sound (biology) from how it was recorded (site, hydrophone, gain) detect unknown marine sounds better than Google's Perch 2.0 and a plain SimCLR encoder? The methodology maps cleanly: raw hydrophone audio windowed into 5-second clips (Signal) → disentangled two-head encoder scoring each window for biological content vs. recording artifact (h_bio / h_site) (Rank) → novelty score + cluster labels surfacing sounds the classifier has never seen (Explain). The computer vision side: spectrograms (log-mel, linear, PCEN) are the feature representation — ResNet18 and TinyCNN operate on them as 2D images, making this a CV problem applied to passive acoustic monitoring.

03Explain

Why the work all looks the same.
Signal → Rank → Explain.

The thread across MedGap, the Options Scanner, the Incident Agent, the Lakehouse, the CFPB research, and Veil is a single methodology. Take a noisy input space. Filter to what deserves attention. Score what survives. Surface the reasoning so a human can act, not just look.

01 · Signal

Filter the firehose.

Most systems evaluate everything and try to sort the noise. The Hans Paradigm inverts: narrow the input first, with cheap deterministic detectors, before any expensive evaluation runs. Universe enters; few-hundred candidates exit.

02 · Rank

Score what survives.

The ranker is permitted to be expensive because the upstream filter has paid down volume. Score by what maps to action: P(profit), AUC, similarity, expected value. Never by what's easy to measure.

03 · Explain

Hand the human a decision.

The output is not a prediction. It is a decision-ready artifact: a labeled action, a drafted RCA, a SHAP-explained risk band, a fairness breakdown. The system shows its work in the register the operator needs.

Disentangled Self-Supervised Learning for Marine Novelty Detection: Separating Biology from Recording Site

Sarthak Hans
FAU DIS Lab · independent research · in progress

Trains a two-head SSL encoder on the DCLDE 2027 killer-whale dataset (~225K bounding-box annotations, 11 providers, 23 locations) to disentangle biological identity (h_bio) from recording-site characteristics (h_site). Benchmarks novelty detection and cross-site generalization against Google Perch 2.0 and a plain SimCLR encoder. Spectrograms (log-mel, linear, PCEN) are processed by ResNet18 and TinyCNN as 2D images. Stages 1–2 (EDA, windowing, augmentations, supervised/pair datasets) complete; stages 3–7 (Perch baseline through full DIS training) in progress on Colab GPU.
